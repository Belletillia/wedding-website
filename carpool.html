Carpool

We get it, travelling from London to Wales without a car is tricky! To help everyone get here, we'd like to help out as much as we can to arrange carpools over to Llanwrtyd Wells.

It's likely to be far easier than the journey by public transport, and sharing lifts helps spread the cost of petrol and car hire too. Plus it's more eco friendly!

If you're planning on driving down, and you'll have a couple of spare seats in your car and you'd be happy to take some extra passengers, please let us know. Equally, if you need a ride, then please fill in the form below so that we can help match everyone up! No pressure to sign-up straight away, as our wedding is still a long way off. However, if you'd like to be involved, please sign up no later than May 31st, 2027, to give us plenty of time to sort things out!
