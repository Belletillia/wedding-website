Getting Around

Our venue is in a lovely remote area, which unfortunately means neither public transport nor Uber (and similar rideshare apps) service the area. We will be providing a shuttle service, but if you are arranging your own transport, please book it well in advance!

Recommended local taxi companies:

Getting to Wales

If you're travelling from outside of Wales, driving is the easiest way to get to Llanwrtyd Wells.

Carpooling

Many of our guests will be travelling from London and the surrounding area, and we know not everyone will have access to a car. To make the journey easier, we'd love to help arrange carpools to the venue the day before. Please see the Carpool page for details.

Airports

For our guests coming from further afield, the nearest options are:

  • Cardiff Airport — roughly a 2 hour drive
  • Bristol Airport — roughly a 2½ hour drive
  • Birmingham Airport — roughly a 3 hour drive

If you'd prefer to fly into London a few days before the wedding to get over your jetlag, that's a good option too! There are six international airports that serve London and you can join in on the carpooling too.
