Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

The dress code is relaxed, with smart or smart-casual clothing encouraged. The ceremony will be outdoors in a country setting, so please choose footwear that is comfortable on grass. The ceremony and reception are at the same venue, so you will not need walking shoes. Black tie is not expected, but please leave trainers and jeans at home.

Are children welcome?

Regrettably, due to number restrictions, we will not be able to accommodate any children other than babes-in-arms.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Our venue has a limited capacity, so invitations are for named guests only. Thank you for your understanding. If you think you've been left out, please get in touch and we'll do our best to help!

Is the ceremony/reception indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be held outdoors. The venue is well prepared for rain, and so if it does rain, we will be in a covered area in the venue's courtyard.

The reception will be held indoors.

Will there be parking available?

There is parking available at the venue, but it is fairly limited. When you return your RSVP, please let us know if you will need a parking spot so that we can arrange overflow parking if necessary.
