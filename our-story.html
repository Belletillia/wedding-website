How It Began

Our Story

We met while working together on a game called Blast Royale in the summer of 2022 and got to know each other by a combo of many Friday night pub sessions with our colleagues, spirited lunchtime convos about whether Dota 2 or League of Legends is a better game, and hosting livestreams together at work.

We worked well together, made each other laugh, and before we knew it, we were spending more and more time together outside of working hours too. Dinners, movies, parties with friends: our friendship deepened as we talked about our lives and the surprising similarities we shared in our upbringing.

Eventually, at the end of the year, the work Christmas party arrived. Emboldened by free booze and terrible karaoke performances, we finally worked up the courage to admit our feelings to one another, and the rest is history.
